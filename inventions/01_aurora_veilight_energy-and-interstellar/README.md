# AURORA / VEILIGHT

This folder groups two related but distinct research architectures that should not be conflated.

**AURORA** is a fault-contained thermal architecture for a hypothetical future fusion plant. Its contribution is the separation of sector heat capture, independent loops, intermediate buffering, power conversion and grid interface. It does not claim a new fusion reaction, eliminate all accident classes, or forecast a net-electric plant output.

**VEILIGHT** is an uncrewed, externally powered, directed-energy lightsail **flyby** research path. It moves the energy source off the vehicle but does not claim crewed relativistic travel, destination braking, mission-grade shielding or a usable interstellar mission.

> **Highest-value VEILIGHT test:** a vacuum sail coupon must outperform a flat control on coupled optical, thermal and beam-capture behaviour.
