# SolarScreen Re-Audit

## Original claim corrected

The original transparent solar/display façade conflated daylight transmission, PV absorption, high-luminance display output, thermal control, serviceability and building qualification. A monolithic energy-positive daytime media façade is rejected as the current central claim.

## Retained mechanism

A patterned semi-transparent BIPV insulated-glass unit can be investigated as a solar-control/vision component when PV, clear area, thermal behavior and power conditioning are measured together. Long-lived glazing and short-lived electronics are separated through a mullion cassette.

## Rejected paths

| Rejected path | Reason |
|---|---|
| “Fully transparent” power-generating vision façade | Photon allocation and glazing/PV optical trade-offs cannot be concealed by terminology. |
| Embedded sealed display matrix as the primary service architecture | It couples display failure to IGU replacement and confuses display energy with PV output. |
| Whole-building or self-powering display claim | Needs location-specific envelope, occupancy, HVAC, duty-cycle and planning evidence. |

## Decisive falsifier

If a candidate IGU cannot show a defensible optical/thermal/electrical trade-off relative to a matched passive or conventional-BIPV baseline, reframe it as non-vision spandrel BIPV or terminate the vision-panel path.

## Unresolved

Long-term laminate/edge durability, project-specific structural/fire/weathering qualification, facade access, glare/planning, maintenance economics and climate-specific building performance remain unresolved.
