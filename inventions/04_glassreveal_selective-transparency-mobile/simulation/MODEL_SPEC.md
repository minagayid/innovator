# GlassReveal Optical-Integration Model Specification

**Evidence class:** finite optical-stack and integration-screening plan. No handset certification, structural, battery, RF, SAR, or product claim follows from this specification.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

A rear selective-reveal laminate can add a controlled, repeatable reveal state over an opaque service cassette while preserving a defined private/default state and remaining outside the main display, thermal, structural and primary-RF functions.

| Case | Input change | Required discriminating outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Fixed printed rear-glass graphic. | Records static optical, mass, thermal and appearance reference. |
| Candidate | Rear reveal laminate over designed cassette. | Produces a measurable intended change in reveal/obscuration state. |
| Negative control | Non-uniform / over-dense stack. | Exceeds the pre-registered haze, non-uniformity or leakage threshold. |

## Measurements and acceptance plan

| Quantity | Measurement method | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Spectral transmission and haze | Spectrophotometry and haze meter across multiple sample positions / angles. | Quantify clear-state quality and lot uniformity. |
| Private-state leakage | Imaging/photometry of a standardized cassette target. | Verify the default state has a clear functional purpose. |
| Switching response and drift | Repeated declared duty cycle at controlled temperature. | Screen electro-optical reliability; do not extrapolate to product lifetime. |
| Thickness/mass | Caliper/micrometer and scale against baseline. | Bound industrial-design penalty. |
| Thermal and functional isolation | Controlled thermal soak followed by later handset-mule RF/camera/sensor comparisons. | Determine exclusion zones; not a compliance result. |

## Outputs

Store raw measurement records, calibration details, sample genealogy, statistical summary, images under matched illumination, and a decision log. The model/pass criterion must be defined before coupon testing. A pass supports only a rear-laminate first article; it does not validate a transparent phone.
