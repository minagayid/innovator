# Scientific review — inventions 01–11

**Review date:** 15 September 2026

**Scope:** Research-package claims, model boundaries, nearest-known mechanisms,
conservation/accounting, safety limits, and the next decisive falsification
step. This is not a systematic review, patent opinion, certification, or
authorization to build or deploy any concept.

| # | Programme | Review decision and material correction | Next decisive gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 | AURORA / VEILIGHT | Keep as separate hypotheses. AURORA's software fault only degrades its selected sector and omits shared-path/common-cause failures; its 405 MWe point is arithmetic only. VEILIGHT's \(2P/c\) ceiling is for a stationary perfect reflector, and its modeled stability follows from assumed restoring/damping terms. | Compare isolated and shared-path thermal rigs with common-cause faults. Measure sail-coupon response; close relativistic beam/sail energy and momentum before mission claims. |
| 02 | TIDEGILL | Gill geometry does not prove gas–sorbent capture. Correct the oxygen-unbalanced legacy cathode equation. Report regeneration heat and electrical energy separately; include conditioning and CO₂ handling. MOXIE's CO/O₂ pathway does not establish elemental-carbon production. | Freeze sorbent/cycle and compare matched contactors; keep the separate carbon cell laboratory-only. |
| 03 | NEREID | Kit swapping does not resolve common capsule mass, center-of-gravity, structure, or certification. The illustrative one-of-eight lift loss drops T/W below the selected threshold. The supervisor now distinguishes pre-entry denial from active fault reporting; its emergency state is not an action. Buoyancy is not vessel stability or pressure-hull evidence. | Weigh each configuration and compare load paths; use interface fault injection and later HIL for state timing. Keep flight and water research uncrewed and independently gated. |
| 04 | GlassReveal | Freeze one powered-clear PDLC stack and polarity. PSLC and PDLC can switch in opposite directions; generic “normally scattering PDLC/PSLC” is underspecified. Provisional optical targets are now explicit and labeled design choices, not standards. | Test transmission, haze, target contrast, colour shift, angular uniformity, latency, energy, and cycle drift against printed-glass controls. |
| 05 | SolarScreen | Keep as a component-level BIPV vision-panel hypothesis. Use current glazing/BIPV methods and separate angular glare measurements; meter PV and parasitic loads independently. Do not infer building savings from coupon performance. | Pre-register geometry, climate/orientation, matched baselines, area fraction, uncertainty, and thresholds; test a representative IGU and service cassette. |
| 06 | TransistorMesh | Local reuse/direct PE communication are established accelerator mechanisms (Eyeriss). The candidate must name a distinct topology/gating/mapping policy and include off-chip memory/I/O energy. No novelty or yield claim is established. | Freeze one policy/workload; compare tuned bus and systolic baselines under a matched PDK, workload, precision, throughput, memory capacity, PVT/SRAM limits, and energy boundary. |
| 07 | Horizon-Weave | Retain the no-build mathematical-study conclusion. The flat control now traverses the calculation path but cannot reveal sign errors because its expected result is zero. The integrated NEC result is quadrature consistency against an analytic profile, not an independent stress-energy derivation. | Test the computed zero-shape control and preserve explicit limits; any independent sign check needs a nonzero reference derived by a separate route. |
| 08 | MYCO-CLEAN | Keep PET-first and contained. The 0.044%/day PE result followed UV pretreatment and is not cleanup throughput. The previous zero “mass-balance error” was guaranteed by defining residue as input minus product; the revised model exposes fraction-derived streams and makes no measured-closure claim. NIH review scope is qualified. | Independently measure solid, soluble, gas, filter, wastewater, particles, products, and enzyme retention/removal in a closed process; compare recoverable product per energy with mechanical recycling. |
| 09 | MEL-SHIELD | Keep as nonliving, spectrum-specific material coupons. A 2020 test found no melanin advantage over cellulose for β-radiation; a 2025 LEO PLA-composite study is narrowly material- and exposure-specific and does not establish human dose-equivalent or GCR protection. Fungal source-material handling is separate from purified-material risk. | Use matched areal-mass controls, declared spectrum/geometry, calibrated dose and secondary-radiation measurements, and aging. |
| 10 | GENO-AQUATIC | Retained separately as a symbolic teaching sandbox. Both shared files labeled GENO-AQUATIC contained NeuroForge, so the missing original was not recoverable. The reconstruction is openly marked and uses fictional labels only; it makes no genome, phenotype, ecology, or safety prediction. | Validate synthetic records and reject sequence, real-species, and operational fields. Review any recovered original before restoring claims. |
| 11 | NeuroForge | Retained as a Parkinson-only, research-only evidence twin. The 2025 iPSC dopaminergic study is a seven-person, single-centre, open-label Phase I/II study with six in efficacy analysis and 24-month follow-up; it reports 73 adverse events and exploratory secondary motor signals. SCI trial evidence is explicitly outside V1. Records now distinguish source kind from study design. | Keep claim-level provenance and uncertainty; human review is required before use outside research. Use current ISSCR guideline v1.2; conference/press items remain preliminary. |
