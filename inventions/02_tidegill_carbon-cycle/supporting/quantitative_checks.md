# Quantitative Feasibility Checks

## Terrestrial fusion-energy concept — proposed planning basis
- Thermal source planning point: 1.00 GW_th.
- Gross electric output at assumed 45% conversion: 450 MW_e.
- Auxiliary allowance at 10% of gross: 45 MW_e.
- Net planning output: 405 MW_e.
- Two-hour full-thermal-output buffer: 7.20 TJ.

## CO2 to elemental carbon + oxygen — ideal lower bound
- One metric tonne CO2: 22,722 mol.
- Theoretical carbon product: 272.9 kg.
- Theoretical oxygen coproduct: 727.1 kg.
- Ideal reversible minimum energy for CO2 -> C + O2: 8.96 GJ = 2.49 MWh per tonne CO2.
- Actual system energy must be higher after capture, overpotential, compression, separations, heat loss, and product finishing.

## Directed-energy interstellar probe — kinetic-energy lower bound
- 1 g dry payload at 10% c: gamma=1.005038; ideal kinetic energy=4.528e+11 J = 125.8 MWh.
- 1 g dry payload at 20% c: gamma=1.020621; ideal kinetic energy=1.853e+12 J = 514.8 MWh.
- 1 g dry payload at 50% c: gamma=1.154701; ideal kinetic energy=1.390e+13 J = 3862.2 MWh.
- 1 g dry payload at 90% c: gamma=2.294157; ideal kinetic energy=1.163e+14 J = 32309.2 MWh.
- These values exclude beam/driver losses, sail mass, shielding, trajectory correction, communications, deceleration, and energy used to create the vehicle.

## Shallow submersion demonstrator — static pressure increment
- At 3.0 m freshwater depth, hydrostatic pressure increment: 29.4 kPa.
- This does not determine hull thickness; buckling, fatigue, penetrations, impact, corrosion, and safety factors govern the actual pressure-hull design.
