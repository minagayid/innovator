# Reversal-Criteria Audit

## Purpose

This audit tests whether the prior **no-build** conclusion can be reversed. A reversal requires more than an exact metric or a negative-energy plot. It requires a documented result for every linked physical obligation.

| ID | Required reversal criterion | Evidence needed | Audit result |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Self-consistent source | A specified action/field content, conserved stress-energy, regularity and parameter regime—not an Einstein-tensor back-solve alone. | **Not satisfied by the prior toy study.** A 2022 exact construction still introduces a self-interacting phantom scalar in the GR formulation; its alternative formulation changes the gravity theory. [1] |
| R2 | Semiclassical negative-energy admissibility | A quantum state/model satisfying applicable quantum-energy restrictions in the claimed regime. | **Not satisfied.** A 2024 review states that quantum energy inequalities restrict construction and examines constraints even for proposed semiclassical wormholes. [2] |
| R3 | Macroscopic scale and tidal safety | A source-specific analysis showing a macroscopic regime with curvature/tidal limits and quantum restrictions jointly satisfied. | **Not satisfied.** The cited stringy-theory analysis supported a conclusion of only a few orders above the Planck scale for its analysed theory. [3] |
| R4 | Dynamical stability/backreaction | Well-posed perturbation or nonlinear evolution with disclosed boundary conditions and error analysis. | **Not tested by the static family.** |
| R5 | Global causal and topological admissibility | Formal causal analysis under stated asymptotics, energy conditions and chronology assumptions. | **Not tested by the static family.** The earlier topological-censorship constraint remains relevant under its hypotheses. [4] |
| R6 | Formation, control, transport and safety | A physical formation/control mechanism plus radiation, navigation, material, and safety evidence. | **No candidate mechanism or evidence.** |

## What was narrowed

The literature search narrows one ambiguity: **exact theoretical solutions exist in specialized classical or modified-gravity models, but an exact solution is not equivalent to a realizable source or a portal.** That distinguishes the previous toy family from a larger class of formal studies, without satisfying any of R1–R6.

## Decision rule

The no-build conclusion changes only if every R1–R6 has evidence adequate to its row and the chain is independently reproduced. Failure or absence in any row preserves the no-build conclusion. The current audit preserves it.
