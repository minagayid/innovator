# Scientific review addendum

The flat-space control now passes a zero-shape profile through the same finite-
difference stress-energy formulas. It checks that this control yields zero
residual; because the reference is zero, it cannot detect a sign error. The
volume-weighted NEC audit integrates the already-derived analytic NEC profile
and compares it with its closed-form integral, so it is a quadrature check,
not an independent stress-energy derivation. Keep the non-operational,
no-build disposition.

Sources: [Morris & Thorne](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.15620),
[topological censorship](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9305017), and
[Ford & Roman](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.53.5496).
