# Re-Audit: Horizon-Weave Portal Topology Lab

**Method:** Claim freeze → source constraints → formal metric target → analytic/control checks → proof-gap audit → decision.

## Original framing and correction

The incoming objective combined worm tunnels, black holes, white holes, and portals as though these objects might form a single maintainable engineering system. The re-audit rejects that inference. A Morris–Thorne wormhole is a conditional classical metric construction with demanding stress-energy assumptions [1]. A black-hole exterior and a white-hole region in an idealized analytic extension are not a demonstrated source, controller, or pair of portal terminals. Topological-censorship and quantum-inequality results supply constraints, not operating instructions.[2] [3]

| Original implied claim | Audit finding | Revision decision | Falsifier or reversal condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| “Maintain a worm tunnel.” | The package can calculate the implied NEC diagnostic of a chosen ansatz but has no physical source or stability model. | Reject as an engineering claim; retain as an analytic toy-model study. | A self-consistent source, stability result, quantum-inequality compatibility, and empirical evidence. |
| “Use black holes and white holes for portals.” | No mechanism links comparison geometries to a controlled traversable route. | Treat them as terminology/comparison only. | A complete separate theory/evidence chain; not a diagram or metric patch. |
| “Run until perfect.” | Perfection is undefined and cannot close missing physics. | Replace with finite verification gates and a proof-gap ledger. | Only independently validated evidence can change a bounded gate. |

## Retained contribution

The retained contribution is a reproducible teaching/research artifact: a zero-redshift static metric family, analytic NEC identity, 48 finite checks, a flat-space negative control, a horizon-lapse comparison, regression tests, output hashes, and cited physical constraints. Its strongest result is implementation verification for its declared equations.

## Final decision

**Reframe to non-operational theory audit.** Do not create energy, material, control, transport, safety, manufacturing, or commercial pathways. The package remains useful because it makes the central obstruction explicit rather than hiding it behind a portal narrative.
