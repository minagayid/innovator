# Proof-Gap Ledger and Decision

## Current decision

**Decision: Reframe.** Retain **Horizon-Weave** only as a non-operational mathematical-research architecture for auditing hypothetical throat geometries. Reject any current claim that a worm tunnel can be maintained, that black/white holes can be used as endpoints, or that a portal can be engineered.

The finite study verifies that the implementation reproduces the expected radial NEC violation of a *chosen* Morris–Thorne metric family. That is not a maintenance mechanism. The source literature places the core obstacle at the stress-energy assumptions: the original traversable construction requires unusual tension/energy behavior [1], topological censorship applies under stated global and null-energy assumptions [2], and quantum-inequality analysis further constrains static geometries of the relevant kind [3].

## Obligations that remain open

| Obligation | Why it matters | Status in this package | Decision-reversal evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical stress-energy source | A metric ansatz needs a self-consistent source, not merely a calculated Einstein tensor. | Not modeled. | A theoretically consistent, experimentally supported source with complete conservation accounting. |
| Semiclassical and quantum-inequality compatibility | Local negative-energy terms are constrained; a classical stress tensor alone is insufficient. [3] | Not tested. | A derivation in a defined quantum field theory satisfying applicable bounds. |
| Dynamical stability and backreaction | Static solutions can fail under perturbation or source response. | Not modeled. | A well-posed perturbative/nonlinear evolution with stable solutions and controlled error. |
| Global causal structure | Traversability, horizons, time shifts, and causal pathologies are global—not a throat-plot result. | Not solved. | Formal causal analysis under disclosed global assumptions. |
| Formation, control, and coupling | A solution’s existence does not provide a creation, control, or connection procedure. | Excluded. | Independently supported physical mechanism; not a simulation-only assertion. |
| Black/white-hole interpretation | Idealized analytic-extension regions are not a demonstrated portal apparatus. [4] | Comparison terminology only. | A separate theory and evidence chain; no such route is supplied here. |
| Human/object transport and safety | Requires every preceding obligation plus radiation, tidal, navigation, and safety evidence. | Prohibited. | No near-term reversal criterion from this package. |

## Falsification and stop conditions

For the code, an analytic sign mismatch, non-refining finite-difference error, nonzero flat-space control, or failure of throat/flare-out checks falsifies the **implementation**. For the proposed physical narrative, the present evidence already fails the required-source and stability gates. Therefore the appropriate stop condition is reached: **do not progress to fabrication, energy budgeting, control design, or transport proposals.**

## Adversarial-audit update

The new symbolic certificate and 48-case quadrature/shape-domain check verify
identities within the prescribed family and test numerical consistency. The
volume integral reuses the analytic NEC profile; it is not an independent
stress-energy derivation. This **narrows an implementation uncertainty only**.
It does not provide a physical source or satisfy any of the R1–R6 reversal
criteria documented in [`REVERSAL_CRITERIA.md`](REVERSAL_CRITERIA.md). The full
audit is recorded in [`ADVERSARIAL_RESULTS.md`](ADVERSARIAL_RESULTS.md).

## Language allowed by this release

The package may say that it contains a cited mathematical review, a prescribed classical metric family, a deterministic finite implementation-verification study, comparison geometries, and an explicit set of unresolved obligations. It must not say that it has maintained a wormhole, controlled a black hole or white hole, invented a portal, attained superluminal travel, or reached a perfect design.
