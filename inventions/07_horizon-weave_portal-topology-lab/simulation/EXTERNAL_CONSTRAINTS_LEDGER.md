# External Constraints Ledger

**Scope:** Classical general-relativistic toy geometries and their mathematical stress-energy requirements. This ledger does not model quantum gravity, cosmological formation, laboratory creation, or a deployable portal.

| ID | Claim unit | Evidence status | Consequence for this package |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Morris and Thorne gave horizon-free, in-principle traversable wormhole solutions, but their throat condition requires radial tension exceeding mass-energy density; they stated that no known material has that property. [1] | **[S] Sourced** | Treat a Morris–Thorne metric as a prescribed toy geometry; do not treat its calculated stress-energy as an available material. |
| C2 | The topological-censorship theorem states that, for the stated globally hyperbolic, asymptotically flat, null-energy-condition setting, causal curves from past to future null infinity are homotopic to trivial curves. [2] | **[S] Sourced** | An accessible classical portal must either violate a theorem assumption or cannot be claimed. The package tests only a NEC-violation diagnostic. |
| C3 | Ford and Roman applied quantum-inequality reasoning to static traversable-wormhole geometries and reported severe constraints, concluding macroscopic traversable wormholes appear very improbable under their analysed setting. [3] | **[S] Sourced** | Do not infer that a negative-energy term in a toy Einstein-equation calculation can be scaled to a macroscopic device. |
| C4 | The maximally extended Schwarzschild geometry contains black-hole and white-hole regions in its idealized analytic extension, but the familiar Einstein–Rosen/Schwarzschild bridge is dynamically non-traversable in standard textbook use. The cited thin-shell work is a distinct model with additional exotic throat matter. [4] | **[S] Sourced** | Model black and white holes as comparison geometries and causal-boundary terminology, not as stabilizers or portal endpoints. |
| C5 | A finite numerical pass, a symbolic derivation, or a clear spacetime diagram does not establish a physical source, stability under perturbations, semiclassical consistency, or a realizable transport device. | **[I] Inferred from C1–C4 and method limits** | Require explicit proof gaps, baseline/control results, and a no-build conclusion. |

## Evidence coverage and stop rule

The source coverage is sufficient for the narrow claim boundary: classical static traversability requires an exotic stress-energy feature in the Morris–Thorne example; standard energy/topology conditions constrain accessible nontrivial topology; quantum-inequality analyses impose further restrictions. The search deliberately stops before speculative quantum-gravity or modified-gravity model catalogues because those would broaden, not close, the proof gap for a usable portal.
