# Clinical-translation boundary

Classification depends on intended use, claims, jurisdiction, and deployment.
A literature and model registry may be treated differently from software that
informs diagnosis or treatment. Changing the interface, marketing language, or
data flow can change that status.

Any future clinical transition requires independent institutional and ethics
review, jurisdiction-specific regulatory consultation, validated quality and
manufacturing processes, clinical evidence, and long-term follow-up. NeuroForge
does not make that determination and never authorizes a therapy.
