# Scientific limitations

- Disease mechanisms and endpoints differ; tracks must remain separate.
- iPSC products vary by donor, clone, differentiation state, batch, and genomic stability.
- Organoids can be immature, avascular, variably sized, and incomplete for aging, immune, and long-range circuitry.
- BBB-on-chip models transport and mechanism; they do not reproduce whole-body pharmacology.
- EV/secretome cargo, purity, biodistribution, and potency can vary.
- Biomaterials add foreign-body, degradation, migration, and interface risks.
- Gene/RNA interventions add off-target, immune, persistence, and reversibility risks.
- Bioelectronics and rehabilitation can confound functional outcomes.
- A functional score does not prove cell replacement or circuit integration.
- Generative models can amplify publication bias, missing data, and spurious correlations.
