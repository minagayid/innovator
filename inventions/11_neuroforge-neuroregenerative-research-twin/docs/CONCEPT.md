# Concept and intended contribution

## Problem

Neuro-regenerative work is fragmented across disease models, cell products,
organoids, barrier models, molecular candidates, rehabilitation, and early
clinical signals. “Generative neurological medication” can mean generative AI
for research, not an autonomous medication generator. The system must preserve
that distinction.

## Invention thesis

NeuroForge V1 is a traceable Parkinson disease research operating system that connects:

1. a disease-specific causal hypothesis;
2. a model ladder from human cells to clinical evidence;
3. a candidate and delivery-context registry;
4. a claim-level evidence graph;
5. generative hypothesis support with uncertainty and abstention; and
6. human-controlled translation gates.

The novelty is the combination of provenance, mechanism-linked readouts,
explicit model-transfer checks, and fail-closed translational controls—not a
claim that the software itself regenerates the brain.

## Modalities represented

Dopaminergic cell replacement is the active V1 modality. Trophic/small-molecule,
extracellular-vesicle/secretome, biomaterial, RNA/gene, bioelectronic, and
rehabilitation records are literature comparators only. A positive result for
one modality or scaffold cannot be silently generalized to another.

## Research outputs

Allowed outputs are literature maps, evidence tables, candidate comparisons,
mechanism-to-readout maps, uncertainty reports, and questions for expert review.
All outputs are hypotheses until an authorized human review outside the system.
