# Evidence taxonomy

Evidence is represented on separate axes so one score cannot hide a safety or
efficacy gap:

- **Mechanism:** unsupported, hypothesis, model-supported, or replicated.
- **Safety:** unknown, partially characterized, characterized in model, or
  independently reviewed.
- **Efficacy:** none, exploratory model signal, preclinical signal,
  investigational human signal, or replicated clinical evidence.

The record also stores study type (human clinical, human observational, animal,
organoid/assembloid, in-vitro, computational, or expert hypothesis) separately
from source kind (peer-reviewed article, conference abstract, press release,
guideline, review, registry, preprint, or synthetic example). It records
publication date, reviewer, negative and conflicting findings, endpoint
definition, model limitations, evidence age, and transferability limits. No
combined “readiness” score is emitted.
