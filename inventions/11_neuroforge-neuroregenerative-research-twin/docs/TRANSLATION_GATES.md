# Translation gates

Every candidate remains a hypothesis until an external authorized review accepts
it. Gates are review categories, not automated proof of safety.

1. Disease-specific mechanism and clinically meaningful endpoint.
2. Product identity, subtype, maturity, purity, and batch provenance.
3. Genomic integrity, residual undifferentiated-cell risk, and potency concept.
4. Tumorigenicity, ectopic differentiation, immune compatibility, and inflammation.
5. Biodistribution, persistence, durability, reversibility, and long-term follow-up.
6. Assay-to-clinical relevance and external-validity assessment.
7. Manufacturing/quality, jurisdiction, consent, and ethics review.
8. Independent reviewer sign-off with dissent preserved.

The system can display “known”, “unknown”, “conflicted”, and “prohibited”. It
cannot display “clinically ready” from an algorithmic score.
