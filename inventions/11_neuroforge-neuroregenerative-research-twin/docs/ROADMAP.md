# Roadmap

1. **Scope lock:** Parkinson disease, dopaminergic target population, explicit endpoint, and evidence question.
2. **Data contracts:** validate schemas, evidence labels, provenance, and red-line fields.
3. **Literature layer:** import public metadata with source URLs, dates, study type, and uncertainty.
4. **Research twin:** compare candidate classes and model systems without optimization or prescription.
5. **Generative review:** produce hypotheses and evidence gaps with mandatory human review.
6. **Translation dashboard:** show safety gates, unknowns, conflicts, and dissent; never authorize progression.
7. **Governance review:** independent neuroscience, stem-cell, ethics, regulatory, and ML-safety review.

Spinal-cord injury, gene/RNA design, EV engineering, biomaterial design,
neuroprosthetic control, and de-novo molecular generation are future separately
reviewed modules, not V1 capabilities.

Success metrics include citation accuracy, provenance completeness, refusal recall,
PII leakage rate, subgroup calibration, reproducibility, reviewer override rate,
and model-drift detection time. Thresholds must be validated empirically.
