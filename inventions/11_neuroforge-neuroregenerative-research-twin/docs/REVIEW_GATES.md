# Review gates

The validator and human reviewers use these fail-closed gates:

1. Exact Parkinson disease scope and dopaminergic cell-replacement context.
2. Source URL, retrieval date, license/consent basis, and dataset/model version.
3. Mechanism-linked endpoint and transferability limitations.
4. Cell identity, maturity, purity, genomic integrity, and potency concept at a
   high level; no manufacturing recipe.
5. Tumorigenicity, immune, inflammation, biodistribution, persistence, and
   durability risks recorded as known/unknown/conflicted.
6. Negative, null, and contradictory evidence preserved.
7. Two independent reviewers required before a record is a research priority.
8. No patient-specific or operational output; prohibited fields are rejected.

Human review changes status only; it never converts the record into a clinical
recommendation or authorizes translation.
