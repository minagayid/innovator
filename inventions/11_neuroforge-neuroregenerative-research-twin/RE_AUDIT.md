# Re-audit — NeuroForge

**Disposition:** Retain as a research-only evidence twin focused on Parkinson
dopaminergic cell-replacement literature. Keep it separate from invention 10.

## Findings

- The shared package correctly bounded V1 to Parkinson disease, but listed a
  spinal-cord-injury trial among evidence anchors. That trial concerns a
  different cell product, disease, anatomy, and endpoints. It is now explicitly
  out of the V1 graph and may only inform a separately reviewed future module.
- A 2025 peer-reviewed Phase I/II Parkinson study is recorded with its source
  kind, publication date, sample sizes, follow-up, event count, endpoints, and
  transferability limits. It was single-centre, open-label, and uncontrolled;
  its efficacy signals were secondary, not proof of clinical efficacy or
  disease modification.
- Evidence records now separate study design from source kind, so conference
  abstracts, press releases, guidelines, reviews, registries, and peer-reviewed
  articles cannot be silently treated as equivalent sources.
- The current ISSCR guideline page identifies August 2025 version 1.2; its
  targeted 2025 update was limited to stem-cell-based embryo models. Use the
  guideline itself rather than its press-release page as the normative source.

No clinical decision, cell-production protocol, patient data, or treatment
recommendation is provided. Human review remains necessary before any evidence
is used outside this research package.

Sources: [Sawamoto et al., Nature (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08700-0),
[ISSCR Guidelines](https://www.isscr.org/guidelines), and the
[ISSCR 2025 update notice](https://www.isscr.org/isscr-news/the-isscr-releases-targeted-update-to-the-guidelines-for-stem-cell-research-and-clinical-translation).
