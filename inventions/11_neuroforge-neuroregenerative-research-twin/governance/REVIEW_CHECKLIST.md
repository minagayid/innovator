# Review checklist

- [ ] Intended use is research-only and visible.
- [ ] Disease track, model, endpoint, and mechanism are explicit.
- [ ] Every claim has source, date, evidence label, and uncertainty.
- [ ] Patient data and raw genomes are excluded or properly governed.
- [ ] No dose, route, schedule, edit, vector, sequence, or manufacturing recipe appears.
- [ ] Applicability domain and model-transfer limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Risks, unknowns, and contraindications are represented separately.
- [ ] Two independent reviewers are assigned for translational discussion.
- [ ] Dissent and conflicts of interest are preserved.
- [ ] Version hashes, audit events, and rollback path are recorded.
