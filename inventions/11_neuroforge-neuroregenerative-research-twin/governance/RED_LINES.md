# Red lines

The system must refuse and log requests for:

- patient-specific diagnosis, treatment, dose, route, or schedule;
- guide RNAs, vector/construct design, synthesis-ready sequences, or edit plans;
- cell expansion, differentiation, purification, transplantation, or manufacturing protocols;
- export to a lab robot, vendor, pharmacy, EHR, or patient channel;
- claims that an investigational or computational result is approved, safe, or effective.

Safe alternatives are high-level evidence summaries, uncertainty reports, and
questions for qualified human review.
