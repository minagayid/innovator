# Re-Audit: MYCO-CLEAN

**Method:** claim freeze → source constraints → biological boundary → device separation → mass-balance gate → reversal criteria.

## Claim corrections

| Original framing | Audit finding | Revision decision |
|---|---|---|
| Release a plastic-eating fungus into the ocean. | Environmental persistence, gene flow, ecological competition, product fate and monitoring are not bounded. | **Reject.** The ocean-facing unit only captures and contains material. |
| One fungus dissolves every plastic. | PET, PUR, PE, PP, PS and PVC have different bond chemistries and evidence maturity. | **Reject.** Use polymer-specific enzyme lanes. |
| Generate an RNA sequence from desired proteins. | A sequence alone does not establish folding, secretion, stability, host compatibility or safety. | **Reframe.** Record functional protein/RNA module cards without nucleotide sequences or construction instructions. |
| Plastic disappears. | Fragmentation, dissolved organics, CO2 and toxic by-products can be missed by mass loss alone. | **Reject.** Require product identification, particle monitoring and carbon/mass closure. |
| Faster means better. | Pretreatment, heating, mixing and purification consume energy and can move risk downstream. | **Reframe.** Compare recoverable feedstock per unit energy against a mechanical baseline. |

## Retained contribution

The retained invention is a **closed-loop recovery architecture** that combines polymer identification, bounded pretreatment, modular biocatalysis, product recovery and fail-closed effluent handling. The non-obvious design choice is the separation of biological discovery/manufacturing from the environmental collection boundary.

## Prior-art boundary

PET biodegradation by *Ideonella sakaiensis*, engineered PET depolymerases, fungal plastic-enzyme reviews, mechanical recycling and advanced recycling are all prior art or adjacent mechanisms [1]–[5]. This package makes no legal novelty, patentability or freedom-to-operate claim. Any protectable contribution would require a separate patent search and claim analysis.

## Final decision

**Continue as a bounded PET research platform; keep polyolefin work exploratory; prohibit environmental release.**
