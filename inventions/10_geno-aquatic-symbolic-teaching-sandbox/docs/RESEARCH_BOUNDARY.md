# Research boundary

GENO-AQUATIC exists to teach careful separation of a hypothesis, its evidence,
and its limitations. Each candidate record uses a fictional aquatic analogue
and named symbolic features; it does not encode an organism's genome or a
molecular design.

## Included

- Synthetic candidate identifiers and hypothetical trait labels.
- Evidence-provenance placeholders and uncertainty statements.
- Discussion of how a conceptual model would need independent validation.
- Offline record validation that rejects sequence-bearing or operational fields.

## Excluded

- Nucleotide or protein sequences, target selection, guide design, constructs,
  vectors, codon optimization, synthesis, transformation, or breeding methods.
- Real species or strain-specific intervention plans.
- Wet-lab, field-trial, animal-use, or environmental-release instructions.
- Claims about fitness, ecosystem impact, food safety, or regulatory approval.

The words “genome consequence” in the broader project context do not make this
toy representation a genomic predictor. Any future real-organism research would
require a new scope, specialist review, applicable institutional and legal
oversight, and evidence not present here.
