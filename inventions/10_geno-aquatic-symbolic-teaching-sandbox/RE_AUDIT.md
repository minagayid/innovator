**Disposition:** Retain as invention 10 and keep separate from NeuroForge
(invention 11). The supplied shared-conversation archives both contained
NeuroForge files, so the original GENO-AQUATIC package was not recoverable.

The task description identified a computational genome-consequence teaching
sandbox. To avoid inventing biological claims from missing files, this
reconstruction uses a synthetic aquatic analogue with symbolic labels only. It
has no organism-specific content, sequences, molecular design, wet-lab or
breeding steps, or release instructions. Its only testable claim is that its
offline validator accepts the fixed synthetic example and rejects sequence,
real-species, and unscoped fields.

This package does not model real genome consequences, phenotype, fitness,
ecology, or safety. A recovered original archive would need a separate review
before any of its scientific claims could be restored.
