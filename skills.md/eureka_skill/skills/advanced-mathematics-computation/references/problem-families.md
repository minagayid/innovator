# Problem-Family Defaults

## Analytic number theory

Use high-precision or ball arithmetic, explicit truncation bounds, zero isolation, and an independent completeness count. Report height, precision, residuals, bracket widths, and endpoint treatment.

## Complexity theory

Name the computational model before measuring anything. Separate verification, exact solving, approximation, proof complexity, circuit complexity, and timing. A finite benchmark cannot establish an asymptotic lower bound.

## Nonlinear PDE

Record the equation, domain, boundary conditions, discretization, time integrator, CFL condition, resolution sequence, conservation laws, and refinement behavior. Treat blow-up indicators as candidates until they persist under refinement and connect to a rigorous estimate.

## Gauge theory

Track group, dimension, action, lattice spacing, physical volume, coupling, thermalization, autocorrelation, observable definition, renormalization, and continuum/infinite-volume extrapolation.

## Arithmetic geometry

Use canonical models, discriminant/conductor, finite-field counts, exact rational linear algebra, height bounds, rank or cycle certificates, and independent implementations of point counts or intersection calculations.

## Differential geometry and topology

Compute exact chain complexes, homology, fundamental-group presentations, triangulation invariants, and curvature-flow diagnostics. Distinguish an invariant computation from a classification theorem.
