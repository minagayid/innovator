# Computational Experiment Plan

## Mathematical target

State the exact proposition, domain, quantifiers, and theorem-level outcome sought.

## Evidence level

Choose one: calibration, finite empirical evidence, certified finite result, research lead, theorem candidate, or proof.

## Computational model

Describe the algorithm, precision, discretization, solver, random seed if any, and resource limits.

## Coverage and bounds

State the finite range, partitioning, stopping rule, and what remains outside the range.

## Controls

List negative controls, resolution/precision checks, independent implementation, invariants, and expected failure behavior.

## Checkpoint and manifest

Specify checkpoint path, resume behavior, machine-readable summary, raw-data path, and hashes.

## Results

Report actual measurements, anomalies, uncertainty, and failed runs. Do not replace missing values with estimates.

## Proof gap

Name the exact mathematical obligation not discharged by the computation.

## References

List authoritative problem statements and methodological sources with direct links.
