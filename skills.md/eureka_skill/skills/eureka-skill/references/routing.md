# Eureka Routing Map

| User need | Primary route | Add when needed |
|---|---|---|
| Ambiguous, high-stakes or contested claim | `critical-creative-thinking` | `adversarial-peer-review` for repeated attacks and repair; `research-synthesis-hypothesis` for external evidence. |
| Peer review, debate preparation, red-team or stress test | `adversarial-peer-review` | Add the domain-specific rubric and `invention-validation-lab` or `eureka-computational-lab` when the work is a proposed invention or quantitative study. |
| Research frontier or opportunity map | `frontier-research-architect` | `contradiction-mechanism-lab`, `cross-domain-recombination`. |
| New invention, theory or product mechanism | `invention-generation-orchestrator` | `conjecture-formalization-lab`, `invention-validation-lab`. |
| Mathematical conjecture or numerical theorem study | `advanced-mathematics-computation` | `eureka-formal-logic-lab` for formal/finite claims. |
| High-integrity numerical/scalable computation | `eureka-computational-lab` | `advanced-mathematics-computation` for theorem-aware method selection. |
| Optimization or architecture trade-off study | `eureka-design-space-lab` | `eureka-computational-lab` for VVUQ and evaluation execution. |
| Physical invention workspace and publication | `bounded-invention-engineering` | `invention-validation-lab`, `adversarial-peer-review`, and the computational/design-space skills as appropriate. |

## Universal stop gate

Do not call an output proved, validated, safe, novel, patentable, commercially viable, or ready to build unless the named evidence process has actually been completed. Preserve the narrower evidence label, versioned artefacts and proof-gap ledger.
