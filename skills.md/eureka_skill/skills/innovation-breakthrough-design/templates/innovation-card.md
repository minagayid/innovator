## Selected concept

Describe the system, equation, workflow, or invention precisely enough to prototype.

## Why it might work

Explain the mechanism and identify the analogous structure transferred from another domain.

## Limiting cases and failure modes

List known regimes where the concept should work, where it should reduce to a baseline, and where it may fail.

## Smallest decisive test

Specify input, procedure, success threshold, and a stop rule.

## Novelty and prior-art boundary

List nearest known approaches and state what is genuinely different without making an unverified legal or scientific claim.

## Decision

Choose: prototype, revise, archive, or reject. Explain why.
