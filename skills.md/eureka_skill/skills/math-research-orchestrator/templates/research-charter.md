# Research Charter

## Goal

State the single outcome this program must produce.

## Success criteria

Define the minimum evidence, artifact, or decision that counts as success.

## Scope and constraints

List included domains, exclusions, compute limits, source date range, and approval boundaries.

## Route

Mark the required passes: critical/creative, research synthesis, innovation design, advanced mathematics computation, adversarial review.

## Initial hypotheses or candidate mechanisms

Record alternatives without treating any as established.

## First bounded iteration

Specify the smallest useful search, derivation, prototype, or experiment, including stop condition.

## Handoff

State the next specialist, requested operation, expected output, and unresolved risk.
