# Design-Space Study Specification

## Decision and claim boundary

State the decision, intended operating regime, excluded claims, and what a favorable result must not be used to establish.

## Design variables and constraints

List variables, domains, units, hard constraints, soft preferences, fixed assumptions, and infeasible regions.

## Objectives and baselines

Define measurable objectives, common baselines, target thresholds, and directions of preference. Do not mix incomparable metrics without a stated trade-off rule.

## Sampling and budget

State the initial design, adaptive/space-filling/sensitivity sampling rule, evaluation budget, seed policy, stopping rule, and treatment of failed evaluations.

## Model credibility

Record numerical/model/measurement fidelity, verification/validation status, surrogate role and held-out checks, and regime limits.

## Sensitivity and robustness

Describe local/global sensitivity, uncertainty ranges, decision-reversal scenarios, and robustness criteria.

## Pareto or trade-off interpretation

Define domination, feasibility, constraint margins, how an apparent frontier will be checked, and which trade-offs require human judgment.

## Reproducibility manifest

Record source revision, input table, software/environment, commands, output hashes, figures and test command.

## Stop condition and proof gap

State what result ends exploration, what result causes reframe/reject, and which physical/formal evidence remains required.
