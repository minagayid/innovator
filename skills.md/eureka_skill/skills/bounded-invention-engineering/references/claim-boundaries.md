# Evidence Labels and Claim Boundaries

Use these labels in design, model, manufacturing, and publication documents.

| Label | Meaning | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| **[S] Sourced** | Directly supported by an identified external reference. | “The source states …” |
| **[I] Inferred** | Follows from stated assumptions, equations, or transparent accounting. | “Under the declared model, …” |
| **[P] Proposed** | A design mechanism or integration hypothesis awaiting test. | “The design proposes …” |
| **[U] Unresolved** | A proof, test, data set, analysis, or approval is missing. | “This remains unresolved pending …” |

## Disallowed upgrades

Do not upgrade a finite simulation to universal proof. Do not upgrade a component pass to system safety. Do not upgrade a concept render to a fabrication drawing. Do not upgrade a roadmap to manufacturing readiness. Do not upgrade a reference list to a novelty or patentability conclusion.

## Minimum proof-gap ledger

For every retained concept, name the next missing item: a model-transfer experiment, resolution check, calibrated parameter, independent replication, hazard analysis, certification basis, supplier qualification, lifecycle account, or physical test.
