# Proposal and Communication Templates

Adapt these templates to the exact brief. Replace bracketed text only with verified facts or questions. Keep every proposal in draft state until the user confirms the exact submission.

## Data analysis / SQL proposal

**Opening**

> Your project calls for [specific analysis or output]. I can help structure the work around [Python/SQL/Pandas/statistics/visualization], with a reviewable first pass before any final recommendations.

**Evidence**

> My current evidence includes [exact CV, LinkedIn, portfolio, credential, or project evidence]. I would use that foundation to [bounded task], while keeping assumptions and limitations visible.

**Method**

> I would begin by checking the schema and data quality, defining the requested metrics, producing a reproducible analysis, and presenting the results with clear tables or visualizations. Any modeling would be treated as interpretable analysis with validation and human review rather than an unsupported production guarantee.

**Questions**

> Before finalizing scope, could you confirm: (1) the database type and sample schema, (2) the required output format, and (3) the acceptance criteria or examples of a successful result?

**Close**

> I would be happy to review the dataset structure and propose a small first milestone. I have not assumed a final deadline or outcome until those details are confirmed.

## Applied AI / workflow proposal

**Opening**

> I can help with a bounded applied-AI workflow focused on [specific use case]. My relevant foundation is [verified Python, Claude API, prompt-engineering, workflow-design, evaluation, or healthcare-operations evidence].

**Method**

> The first phase would define the inputs, expected outputs, review criteria, failure cases, and human approval points. I would then prepare a small test set, compare outputs against the agreed rubric, and document limitations and next-step options.

**Scope boundary**

> I would not represent this as production cloud deployment, expert RAG/LangChain implementation, regulated medical-device validation, or a guaranteed business result unless the required environment, evidence, and acceptance criteria are supplied and agreed.

**Questions**

> Please confirm the available tools or API access, data sensitivity requirements, example inputs and desired outputs, and who will approve the results.

## Clarifying message before proposal

> Hello [client name]. I reviewed the brief for [project]. The strongest match with my current experience is [specific task]. Before I draft a precise scope, could you confirm [question 1] and [question 2]? I want to make sure the deliverable, data access, and acceptance criteria are aligned before discussing timing or price.

## Client-reply review template

Before sending a reply, summarize:

| Item | Review |
|---|---|
| Client’s request | Quote or paraphrase the requested change. |
| New scope | Identify added deliverables, data, tools, revisions, or rights. |
| Commercial impact | Note price, milestone, platform fee, or payment changes. |
| Timeline impact | Mark any new deadline or dependency; do not accept it automatically. |
| Risk | Identify sensitive data, unsupported claims, unpaid work, or off-platform requests. |
| Draft reply | Write a concise response and leave it unsent. |

## Contract and delivery checklist

Before contract acceptance, ask the user to review deliverables, acceptance tests, milestones, payment terms, revision limits, rights, confidentiality, data access, and platform rules. Before delivery, prepare a QA summary, known limitations, file list, and draft handoff message. Send only after the user confirms. Record payment as unverified until an official platform status or user-provided receipt confirms it.
