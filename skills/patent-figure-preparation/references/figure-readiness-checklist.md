# Patent-oriented figure readiness checklist

This is a technical handoff checklist, not a substitute for official patent-office rules.

## Scope and source
- [ ] Jurisdiction and application type are recorded, or the set is labelled a general concept draft.
- [ ] The embodiment and known variants are described.
- [ ] Every important feature is traceable to the description or a supplied/source reference.
- [ ] Captions do not imply unsupported performance or legal conclusions.

## Drawing quality
- [ ] Each figure has a unique label and concise purpose.
- [ ] Reference numerals were assigned in the register before rendering labels.
- [ ] Numerals and names agree across all views.
- [ ] Section plane/direction is shown where useful and matches the section view.
- [ ] Exploded order and interfaces are plausible.
- [ ] Lines, hatching, leaders, arrows, and labels remain legible at final size.
- [ ] Marketing scenery and decorative effects are removed from formal-style figures when not needed.
- [ ] Dimensions are useful, supported, and tied to units and datum.
- [ ] Image-model artifacts are corrected or flagged as unresolved.

## Cross-reference and disclosure
- [ ] Every visible callout exists in the register.
- [ ] Registered components are shown where appropriate or noted as unused.
- [ ] Text description and figures show the same embodiment.
- [ ] No figure implies an unmentioned feature that alters the mechanism.
- [ ] Interfaces and steps are not obscured by an unhelpful view.

## Filing handoff
- [ ] Current official guidance for the target office and route was checked.
- [ ] Format, margins, sheets, and line-quality requirements were checked against that guidance.
- [ ] Counsel has reviewed claim support and intended protection strategy.
- [ ] Open items have an owner and next action.
- [ ] The handoff states that the drawings remain a draft until review is complete.
