# Requirements and verification matrix

Give every requirement a stable ID. Requirements should be testable or explicitly marked as not yet measurable. Keep verification separate from validation of whether the concept solves the intended problem.

## Review questions

- Is the requirement observable rather than a vague benefit?
- Are unit, test context, baseline, and threshold explicit?
- Is the threshold sourced, derived, or proposed—and why?
- Could another factor mask failure?
- Is a negative control, boundary case, or off-nominal test required?
- Is evidence reproducible, versioned, and linked to inputs?
- What does this test **not** establish?
